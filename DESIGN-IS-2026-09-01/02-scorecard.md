# Dieter Rams Scorecard

1. Good design is innovative — Score: 1/3
   Evidence: Conventional Bootstrap/task-table patterns dominate (§1).
   Justification: It imitates familiar Todo UI without a clear interaction improvement.
2. Good design makes a product useful — Score: 2/3
   Evidence: All primary actions are on one surface, but manual refresh adds friction (§2).
   Justification: The task completes directly, though adjacent chrome adds steps and attention.
3. Good design is aesthetic — Score: 1/3
   Evidence: Irregular layout, mixed scales, and large unused viewport area (§3).
   Justification: More than three visible inconsistencies prevent a coherent system.
4. Good design makes a product understandable — Score: 1/3
   Evidence: Generic `Product`, English placeholder, and mixed-language caption (§4).
   Justification: Multiple controls or labels require interpretation on a Japanese screen.
5. Good design is unobtrusive — Score: 2/3
   Evidence: Header, refresh, filters, and row actions remain visually persistent (§5).
   Justification: The chrome is functional but does not fully recede behind tasks.
6. Good design is honest — Score: 2/3
   Evidence: No dark patterns or inflation; one generic destination label (§6).
   Justification: Behavior is honest with one minor labeling mismatch.
7. Good design is long-lasting — Score: 1/3
   Evidence: Default Bootstrap navbar/table/button styling lacks durable tokens (§7).
   Justification: Several dated markers tie the UI to an older admin-template aesthetic.
8. Good design is thorough — Score: 2/3
   Evidence: All states exist, but focus styling is largely browser-default (§8).
   Justification: One important state is present but visually rough.
9. Good design is environmentally friendly — Score: 2/3
   Evidence: 52KB gzip JS and no idle motion; no dark-mode preference (§9).
   Justification: Weight and motion are restrained, but system preferences are incomplete.
10. Good design is as little design as possible — Score: 1/3
    Evidence: Ten controls for one task, including removable refresh/decorative chrome (§10).
    Justification: Three or more elements can be removed or demoted without breaking the task.

Total: **15/30**
